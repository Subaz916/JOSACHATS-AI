Frequently asked questions

Can I use my own business documents?

Yes. Add PDF, DOCX, TXT, URLs, FAQs, products, and services from the dashboard.

Does the agent work offline?

Text support requires server APIs. Voice uses device voices where available and clearly reports when a local voice is unavailable.

Which payment gateway is supported?

Safepay Express Checkout v3 in sandbox mode, with signed webhooks and server-side activation.

Can customers reach a human?

Yes. Human support requests appear in the dashboard with customer contact details and transcript context.
